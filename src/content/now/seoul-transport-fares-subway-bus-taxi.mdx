Seoul is a city you can cross for the price of a coffee, at almost any hour, without ever looking at a timetable. Nine lines under the centre alone, buses colour-coded by how far they go, taxis that arrive in under two minutes and take card. For a lot of visitors this is the first thing about Korea that genuinely surprises them — not a monument, the transport.

It is also where people quietly overpay, because two or three rules are never explained at the gate.

Every fare below comes from the City of Seoul's own published tables, **checked on 14 August 2026**. Subway fares are the schedule effective 28 June 2025; bus fares are effective 12 August 2023; the standard taxi meter is the schedule effective 1 February 2023.

Start with the thing that costs people money quietly: **paying without a transport card.**

## Get a card. The gap is on every single ride

| Subway, one ride | With a card | Single-journey ticket |
|---|---|---|
| Adult | **₩1,550** | ₩1,650 |
| Youth (13–18) | **₩900** | ₩1,650 |
| Child (6–12) | **₩550** | ₩550 |

An adult pays **₩100 more per ride** without a card. A teenager pays **₩750 more** — the single-journey ticket has no youth rate at all.

That is before the larger loss: **transfers between bus and subway are free only with a card.** Pay separately each time and a two-leg journey costs two full fares.

The card also unlocks the **early-bird discount**: tap your first ride before 06:30 and the base fare drops 20%, to ₩1,240 on the subway.

## Bus fares by type

Seoul runs several bus classes and they are not priced alike. The number and colour on the bus tell you which.

| Bus type | Card | Cash |
|---|---|---|
| **Blue / Green** (간선·지선) | **₩1,500** | ₩1,500 |
| **Circular** (순환) | ₩1,400 | ₩1,400 |
| **Red — wide-area** (광역) | **₩3,000** | ₩3,000 |
| **Late-night** (심야) | ₩2,500 | ₩2,500 |
| **Village / neighbourhood** (마을) | ₩1,200 | ₩1,200 |

The trap is the **red bus at ₩3,000** — double a normal city bus. Red buses run to satellite cities outside Seoul. If one arrives first and you are travelling inside Seoul, you have paid twice the fare for no benefit.

## The distance surcharge nobody mentions

Base fare covers the first **10 km**. After that:

- **10–50 km**: +₩100 for every 5 km
- **Beyond 50 km**: +₩100 for every 8 km

For most journeys inside Seoul this is nothing — a market run, a [palace day](/en/guides/five-palaces-seoul-which-one/), an evening in Hongdae all sit inside the base fare. It matters when you go out to Incheon, Suwon or the airport — a long metro run can add several hundred won you did not expect.

## Taxis

The standard medium taxi, on the schedule effective 1 February 2023:

| | |
|---|---|
| Base fare | **₩4,800** for the first 1.6 km |
| Distance | +₩100 per 131 m |
| Time (below 15.33 km/h) | +₩100 per 30 s |

Deluxe and large taxis are a different schedule: **₩7,000** for the first 3 km, then ₩100 per 151 m or per 36 seconds.

Two things that surprise visitors:

**There is no tipping.** None is expected and rounding up is not customary.

**Late-night surcharge applies** in the small hours, and travel outside the city boundary can carry an intercity surcharge. Both are added by the meter, not negotiated.

## Airport buses

Limousine buses to Incheon are priced by class, not by distance:

| Route | Standard | Deluxe |
|---|---|---|
| Seoul ↔ Incheon Airport | ₩10,000 | ₩16,000 |
| Seoul ↔ Gimpo Airport | ₩5,000 | ₩8,000 |
| Gimpo ↔ Incheon Airport | ₩5,000 | ₩8,000 |

Some deluxe services on the non-Gimpo route run at ₩17,000–18,000. Cash and card are the same price.

## Who rides free

On the subway: **children under 6** (up to three accompanied by an adult, who pays normally), **people aged 65 and over**, registered disabled passengers, and holders of national merit status.

On buses the free category is narrower — under-6s accompanied by an adult.

The over-65 exemption is worth knowing about because it is generous and applies at the gate, not by application.

## The two rules that cost money

**Tap out. Every time.** Seoul Transportation Corporation applies a penalty for failing to tap off: the base fare — ₩1,550 for an adult — is charged again on your next journey. It is not a fine you can dispute at a counter; it lands automatically.

**Youth and child rates require registration.** The discounted fares above are not automatic. The card has to be registered as a youth or child card first, through T-money, or the adult rate is charged.

## What this article does not claim

Fares are as published by the City of Seoul on the date shown above. Korean transport fares change by administrative decision and the subway base fare has already moved once in the last two years — confirm on the city's page before you rely on an exact figure for budgeting.

We have not verified fares for the AREX airport railway, KTX, intercity coaches, or any operator outside the Seoul metropolitan fare system. Those are separate schedules set by separate operators, and we will cover them separately rather than guess here.
