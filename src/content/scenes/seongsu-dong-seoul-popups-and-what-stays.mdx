Two minutes, or thirty-eight minutes. Both of those are walks from Seongsu Station, and which one you get
depends entirely on which "Seongsu" someone meant when they told you to go.

<MapLinks ko="성수동" en="Seongsu-dong" note="Seongdong-gu, Seoul. Three stations serve different parts of it, and they are not interchangeable." />

We should say at the top what this article is not. Seongsu 성수 *(seong-su)* is the neighbourhood where
Korean brands and K-pop acts put their pop-up stores — 팝업스토어 *(pap-eop-seu-to-eo)* — and that is almost
certainly why you have heard of it. **We could not verify a single pop-up from a primary source.** They are
private, temporary, frequently reservation-only, and the one you saw on Instagram in the week you booked
your flight will very likely be dismantled by the week you land. Anyone who publishes a list of them in
English is publishing something that expires.

So this is the other half of the answer: what is there on an ordinary Tuesday, and the thing that will
actually cost you your afternoon.

## The station is the whole problem

We measured these ourselves on KakaoMap on 17 August 2026, walking mode, station centre to the map's pin
for each destination.

| From | To | Distance | Walk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seongsu Station (Line 2) | Handmade Shoe Street | 115 m | 2 min |
| Seongsu Station (Line 2) | Seoul Forest café strip | 1,483 m | 24 min |
| Seongsu Station (Line 2) | Seoul Forest (park) | 2,414 m | 38 min |
| Seoul Forest Station (Suin–Bundang) | Seoul Forest (park) | 1,034 m | 17 min |
| Ttukseom Station (Line 2) | Seoul Forest (park) | 1,734 m | 27 min |

Read the differences, not the absolute numbers. Every route above ends at a single pin, and Seoul Forest
covers 480,994 m² — so the nearest gate is a good deal closer than the figure suggests, whichever station
you use. What the table tells you reliably is the gap: **Seoul Forest Station puts you about 1.4 km nearer
the park than Seongsu Station does**, and Ttukseom sits roughly 700 m behind Seoul Forest Station.

The station named after the neighbourhood is the wrong one for most of what visitors go to Seongsu for.
That is the sentence to remember.

## "Seongsu café street" is not one street

Here two sources we trust disagree, and we would rather show you that than smooth it over.

Ask KakaoMap for 성수동 카페거리 — Seongsu-dong café street — and it resolves the phrase to **서울숲카페거리**,
Seoul Forest Café Street, up beside the park. Ask the Korea Tourism Organization, whose photo listing for
성수동 카페거리 is the source of the café photograph further down this page, and its registered address is
**성수동2가** — the Seongsu Station side, more than a kilometre away.

Neither is wrong. There are two café clusters, and the name floats between them. Which means: if a friend
or a blog tells you to go to "the Seongsu café street", that instruction does not contain enough
information to act on. Ask which station.

## What is open on a Tuesday: the shoe street

The one thing at Seongsu Station that is genuinely two minutes away is the handmade shoe district —
수제화거리 *(su-je-hwa-geo-ri)*. Shoemaking workshops, material suppliers and small showrooms are packed
into a band running from Seongsu Station toward Ttukseom Station, reachable from Seongsu exits 1 to 4.

It is a working trade district, not a heritage exhibit. Most of what you see through the windows is stock
and machinery. The visitor-facing piece is the **성수 수제화 희망플랫폼** *(Seongsu Sujehwa Huimang
Peullaetpom)*, the Hope Platform — showroom on the ground floor, a workshop space above it, listed at
10:00–19:00 and closed Sundays and public holidays.

Two shared shop names you will actually see on signs: **SSST** and **fromSS**, the joint outlet of the
local shoemakers' association.

<Figure src={fromss} alt="A shopfront framed in a red steel container structure signed from SS SEONGSU, with shelves of shoes inside and a bicycle parked at the kerb"
        caption="The fromSS shopfront — a red steel frame, shelves of finished shoes, a bicycle at the kerb. The association sign is the reliable way to tell a shared outlet from a private workshop."

We are deliberately not quoting the figure you will find elsewhere for what share of Korea's handmade shoe
production sits here. It appears in a citizen-reporter piece on Seoul's city portal with no underlying
statistic attached, and we could not find one. It is probably a large share. We are not going to put a
number on it.

## Seoul Forest, and a contradiction on its own page

The park is free, open around the clock, and open every day of the year — the enclosed facilities inside it
are the ones with hours.

park's own description puts at 3 cm deep, which is why the reflection reads as cleanly as it does.

The site has been a royal hunting ground, Seoul's first waterworks from 1908, a racecourse and a golf
course before it opened as a park on 18 June 2005. The horse sculpture near the entrance is there for the
racecourse, not for decoration.

## The container complex at the park entrance

At 왕십리로 63, on the approach to Seoul Forest, there is a complex built from shipping containers signed
**UNDER STAND AVENUE**. It is unmistakable and it is in every set of Seongsu photographs, including ours.

<Figure src={containers} alt="Orange and dark green shipping containers stacked into a building, with UNDER STAND AVENUE painted in white capitals across the orange face"
        caption="The container complex on the Seoul Forest approach. We are showing you the sign so you can recognise it — we are not telling you when it opens, because we could not confirm that."
        {...UNDERSTAND} />

We tried to confirm its current opening hours from its own site and from the Seongdong-gu district tourism
page that lists it. **Both returned empty pages to us on 17 August 2026.** Secondary write-ups give hours;
we are not passing those on, because a container complex's hours are exactly the kind of detail that
changes quietly. Look it up on the day, or treat it as something you walk past on the way to the park.

## If a pop-up is the reason you are going

Three things that are true regardless of which pop-up it is.

**Many require a reservation, and the reservation systems are Korean.** Booking usually runs through a
Korean platform, and the payment and identity walls that stop foreign visitors elsewhere apply here too —
see [why your card is declined online in Korea](/en/now/korea-3d-secure-card-declined-online/).

**Weekends are the queue.** A weekday morning is a different experience of the same street.

**Check the day before, not the week before.** Pop-up runs are commonly measured in days.

And if the pop-up has closed by the time you arrive — which is the ordinary outcome, not the unlucky one —
you are still standing in a shoe-manufacturing district next to a 480,000 m² park, and that was the durable
half of the trip anyway.

## What this article does not claim

We did not visit on the day of writing. The walking distances are KakaoMap's routing, measured by us on
2026-08-17 from station centre to a single destination pin; real walks vary with which exit you use, and
Seoul Forest is large enough that its nearest gate is closer than the table implies.

Seoul Forest's hours come from the Seoul Metropolitan Government's own park page, checked 2026-08-17. The
visitor-centre hours are contradictory in that source and we have left them unresolved rather than guess.

The shoe street details — the Hope Platform's 10:00–19:00 and its Sunday and public-holiday closure, and
the extent of the street — come from a citizen-reporter feature published on Seoul's city portal, not from
a district government notice. We could not open a district source to confirm them. Treat them as likely
rather than certain, and expect individual workshops to keep their own hours.

We could not open the official page for UNDER STAND AVENUE or the Seongdong-gu listing for it, so no
opening hours for it appear here.

Nothing in this article is a claim about any specific pop-up store, brand, artist or shop, and no
individual pop-up was verified. We have filed this under Scenes because K-pop and brand pop-ups are what
bring most foreign readers to Seongsu; the neighbourhood facts above stand on their own sources
independently of that.
