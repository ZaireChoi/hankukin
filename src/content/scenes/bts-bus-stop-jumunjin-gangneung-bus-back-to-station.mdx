Forty-seven stops. That is how far bus 300 carries you from Gangneung Station to the sand where
the *You Never Walk Alone* bus stop stands — and it is the easy half of the day. The hard half is
the one nobody writes about: the bus that brings you back runs under the same number with a suffix,
and it does not stop at the station.

## What is actually standing on the beach

The shelter is a replica. A temporary bus stop was put up on Jumunjin beach for the 2017 album
photographs and taken down when the shoot was over. In June 2018 Gangneung City built it again
in the same spot, 3.8 metres wide and 2.6 metres high, and said in plain terms what it was for:

The Korea Tourism Organization's listing, which credits Gangneung City Hall as its source, says the
same thing more briefly — *made temporarily for the shoot, removed, recreated as a photo zone for
visitors* — and adds the practical lines that matter: open at all hours, no closing day, admission
free, parking free. The English page on the same site carries the address and the phone number of
the Jumunjin tourist information centre but drops the hours and the parking line. Read the Korean
one if you can.

One thing neither page tells you. The KTO database lists this spot twice, under two names and two
addresses — once as 주문진읍 BTS 앨범사진 촬영지 with the village address, once as BTS 버스정류장
with a road address, 주문북로 222-30. The two pins are 146 metres apart. Map apps that draw on KTO
data can therefore show two BTS bus stops. There is one. It is on the sand, at the north end of the
beach, where the Hyangho lagoon meets the sea.

## Getting there: one train, one bus

From Seoul the route is the same one as for the [Goblin breakwater](/en/scenes/goblin-jumunjin-breakwater/):
KTX-Eum from Seoul Station to Gangneung Station, then a city bus north. The fares below are what
Korail's booking screen showed on 5 September for the next day's trains, and what the city's bus
information system publishes.

| Leg | What | Cost | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seoul Station → Gangneung Station | KTX-Eum, 1 h 51 min – 2 h 11 min depending on the train | ₩27,600 standard · ₩33,100 first class | 2026-09-05 |
| Gangneung Station → 주문진해변 stop | City bus 300 toward 향호리, about 47 stops | ₩1,530 card · ₩1,700 cash | 2026-09-05 |
| 주문진해변 stop → the shelter | On foot, toward the sea | Free | — |

Two things about the train. It was a Sunday list we read, and by the evening before, one of the
first ten departures had no seat left at all and first class was gone on five of them. Book before
you get to Seoul Station, not at it. And the KTX-Eum is a different train from the
[KTX to Busan](/en/now/seoul-to-busan-ktx-srt-integration-2026/) — about half the fare, and it
leaves from the same Seoul Station.

Two things about the bus. Gangneung's fare page lists two tiers, standard *(일반, il-ban)* and
seated *(좌석, jwa-seok)*, without saying which routes are which; we have quoted the standard fare
and given you the seated one above so that ₩2,400 in cash covers either case. And the card discount
is real — 10% off, plus a free transfer within an hour, twice — so load a
[T-money](/en/now/seoul-transport-fares-subway-bus-taxi/) before you leave Seoul.

The number to match against the bus display is **300**, and the stop you want is **주문진해변**
*(Ju-mun-jin hae-byeon)*, second from the end of the line. The terminus, 향호리 *(Hyang-ho-ri)*, is
one stop further and also walkable, but 주문진해변 puts you at the beach car park with the shelter
in sight. On the way in the bus passes 영진입구 *(Yeong-jin ip-gu)*, the stop for the Goblin
breakwater, and 주문진항입구 for the fish market. Both are on the way; neither costs a second fare
if you re-board within the hour.

## Getting back: this is the part that goes wrong

<ChartBtsBusStopReturn caption="Bus 300 runs from Gangneung Station to the beach. The buses that run the other way — 300-1 to Anmok, 300-2 to the industrial estate — go back into the city on a different street and pass Gangneung Station without stopping. The only return service that stops across from the station is a separate 300-1(강릉역), three times a day." />

We read the stop lists for all four services on Gangneung City's bus information system on
5 September, and this is what they say.

Bus **300** — depot to 향호리, 43 runs a day, first 06:15, last 21:25 — stops at 강릉역. Bus
**300-1** — 향호리 to Anmok coffee street, 40 runs — does not. Bus **300-2** — 향호리 to the
industrial estate, 41 runs — does not either. Both return services turn off before the station and
run through 용지각 *(Yong-ji-gak)*, 동남아파트 and onward. The stop list of a fourth service,
**300-1(강릉역)**, includes 강릉역건너편 *(Gang-neung-yeok geon-neo-pyeon, across from Gangneung
Station)* — and that one runs three times a day, first at 08:45, last at 17:05 from 향호리.

So the practical answer is this. Take whatever 300-1 or 300-2 comes, get off at **용지각**, and
walk. Kakao Map puts the walk from Yongjigak to Gangneung Station at about 830 metres and fourteen
minutes; that is the map's estimate, not the city's, and it is flat. If you happen to be at the stop
when a bus marked **300-1(강릉역)** pulls in, take it — it is the same fare and it drops you across
the road from the station. Do not wait for it on purpose. Three a day, and the last one has left the
beach before five.

The last regular buses leave 향호리 at 21:10 (300-1) and 21:30 (300-2). Those are departures from the
terminus one stop north of you; check the display at 주문진해변 rather than trusting the minute.

## The bus stop that is a bus stop

Stand at the shelter for a while and you will see it happen: someone sits on the bench and waits.
The city's own line in 2018 was that visitors should enjoy waiting for a bus that never comes, and
that is exactly what the structure is. The stop that buses actually serve — 주문진해변, ID 4200182
on the city system — is on the road behind the beach, a short walk inland, and it is served by
six routes: 시티1, 300, 302, 314, 315 and 911. If you are checking a real-time arrival app, that is
the stop to search for; the photo zone has no ID because no bus goes to it.

The same city system, incidentally, has two stops called 더글로리촬영지 — *The Glory* filming
location. Gangneung names bus stops after dramas. Search it for BTS and you get nothing.

## What this article does not claim

We did not measure the walk from the road to the shelter, or time the bus from the station; the
"about 47 stops" is a count of the published stop list, and we have not seen a journey time from the
operator. The two fare tiers are quoted because the city's page does not say which routes carry
which. Train fares are for one date and one direction, read from the booking screen, not from a
tariff. The album's identity and the replica's size come from the city as reported by two
newspapers, not from the label. We have not used a photograph of the shelter because we do not have
one we are entitled to use; the drawing above is our own, from the city's stop lists.
